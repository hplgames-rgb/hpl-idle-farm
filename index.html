Field rhythm

Each field has its own manager. Assign one to automate that plot, then activate its perk when you want a timed boost. You can also plant and harvest each field manually.

Each plot upgrades separately: add farmers for faster crop cycles or improve its yield. Barn hauling and market delivery keep running as separate jobs.

Tap the barn or Market for one trip, or automate those routes independently.
