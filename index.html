Field rhythm

Tap a field to plant, wait for its crop to grow, then tap again to harvest into its crate.

Tap the barn to send the hauler down to the crop crates and back up to unload. His first carry limit is 10 produce; hire automation or upgrade his capacity as the farm grows.

Tap the Market to send the truck left to the barn. It returns with the produce, which is sold when it gets back.
